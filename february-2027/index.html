The practical problem

Where does the work stall?

A job can lose time while a crew waits for equipment, fieldwork waits for a decision, or the same people are spread across several jobs. The course uses forestry cases to examine those delays and decide which changes will make the work move more reliably.

Bring a project you know. Use it to practice identifying the chain of work that governs the finish date, protecting that date with buffers, and deciding when another job is ready to start.
